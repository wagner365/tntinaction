FAQ

Questions orgs ask

Does my org have to pay anything upfront?

No. Members pay for their own items on your pre-order page. If the order doesn't reach the minimum, everyone is refunded in full.

What's the minimum?

One piece. Orders under 12 use digital print. At 12 or more of the same design we screen print, which lowers the price per piece.

How long does it take?

Mockups in about 24 hours. After your order closes, plan on about 8 business days to delivery. Need it sooner? Ask about rush.

Can you print our university's logo or Greek letters?

University logos and Greek letters are trademarks that need a license to print. We design around your org's own name and artwork, and we'll tell you up front if something in your design needs a license.

How does fundraising get paid out?

After the order closes and is delivered, we send your org its earnings along with a full report: every item, size and dollar.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Ask for a blank sample in any garment we carry so you can check the fit and feel before members order.
