# Beta program: your first 3–5 clubs

**What "pilot" means:** before the public launch, run real orders for a few friendly clubs. You get proof (photos, a testimonial, a real order page), you find out what breaks, and you learn your true costs. They get a deal for being first.

## Who to ask
Pick clubs where you know someone, and where nobody is already locked into South by Sea:
- **Side Hustle Club** and **Personal Finance Club** (you're a member)
- A **club sports** team (lacrosse, hockey, golf, since you play)
- An **intramural** team, a **dorm/floor**, or a **business school org**

## The offer (you decide the numbers)
"Founding Club" deal for the first 5 orgs:
- Free custom design, with unlimited revisions
- Prices at cost +20% instead of +40%, **or** a bigger fundraiser cut
- In exchange: a group photo in the merch, a 2-sentence testimonial, and 10 minutes of feedback

## Message to send (DM or text)
> Hey [name]! I'm starting a custom merch service for clubs at Miami, run through my apparel brand The Northern Threads. I'm picking 5 founding clubs to do discounted orders for while I get it off the ground. You'd get a free custom design, your own order page where members pick sizes and pay, and I deliver everything to your meeting. Would [club] want merch this semester?

## Run each beta order like this
1. Get the design idea, then send a mockup within 24 hours.
2. Create the Square items and payment link, then the store file (see README).
3. Send the order page link and post the deadline in their group chat.
4. Close the order, run `npm run sizes`, place the order with the supplier, and deliver.
5. Afterward, ask:
   - What almost stopped you from ordering?
   - Was the price fair compared to what you've paid before?
   - How was ordering on the page? Anything confusing?
   - Would you order again, and who else should I talk to?
