## Three ways to work with them
1. **Contract printing (best margins).** You buy the blanks wholesale and drop them off, and they only print. You pay per print, not per shirt.
   - Contract pricing usually starts around **72 pieces per design**. Screen fees run about **$25–40 per color**, and turnaround is about **7–10 business days**.
   - Some shops won't do this for small resellers yet. Ask anyway.
2. **Reseller / wholesale rate (easiest).** They supply the shirt and print it, and give you a discount off retail because you bring repeat business. Ask for **15–25% off their retail price list**.
3. **Retail (fallback).** Order like a normal customer and mark it up. Thin margin. Use it only for rush jobs or licensed orders.

## What you need before asking for reseller pricing
- **Ohio vendor's license**, so you can collect sales tax on merch you sell. Apply through the Ohio Business Gateway; the fee is about $25. **Verify the current process and fee.**
- **Blanket sales tax exemption certificate** (Ohio form STEC B). You hand this to suppliers so you don't pay sales tax on goods you resell.
- **Wholesale blank accounts** (S&S Activewear, SanMar). They need the same vendor license.
- A simple **business card or one-pager** (The Northern Threads logo, email, Instagram) so you look like a repeat customer, not a one-off.

> Note: once you're selling merch to clubs, you generally owe Ohio sales tax on those sales whichever supplier you use. Get the vendor's license before the first paid order, and ask an accountant or the Miami small business resources if unsure.

## Questions to ask on the first call
1. Do you offer contract printing or reseller pricing? What's the minimum?
2. Can you send your price list (per-print by quantity and colors)?
3. Screen/setup fees per color? Do you keep screens for reorders?
4. Standard turnaround? Rush options and cost?
5. Can I supply my own blanks? Any brands you prefer or refuse?
6. What file format do you want artwork in (vector .AI/.SVG/.PDF)?
7. Do you do individual name sorting/bagging?
8. How do you handle misprints or defects?
9. Payment terms: deposit upfront, or pay on pickup?

## Protecting your customers
- **Keep the client relationship yours.** You talk to the club. The printer only sees your purchase order.
- Ask for **"blind" packing**, with no shop branding on boxes or bags, if they'll deliver directly.
- Get **pricing in writing** before quoting a club, so your margin is locked.

## The licensing play
Miami logos and Greek letters need licenses that you don't have. **Lebowski already has both.** If a club insists on Miami marks, you have two options:
- **Refer the order** to Lebowski for a referral fee. Ask whether they pay one.
- **Run the order through Lebowski** as the licensed printer. You keep the design work and client service, they print, and the license royalty is built into their price.

Either way you keep the club happy instead of saying no, and you get a door into the Greek market that South by Sea dominates.

## When to switch from DTF to a local screen printer
Run `npm run compare` with a real quote in the `local-screen` section of `data/pricing.json`. Rough rule: **one design, 48+ pieces, 1–2 colors → get a screen print quote.** Below that, DTF is usually cheaper.
